become the optimizer-owned base mesh in fresh MoGe coordinates with identity residual
scale/rotation/translation, and the rebound hand must enter through a fresh compact
external-anchor packet. Static audit must prove override -> capture -> loss ->
backward -> optimizer step. Capture-only execution must record zero updates.

authorized. Joint flow and Gate D remain closed. The five-update review must reject
scale drift, silhouette/depth regression, hand motion, or new hand-only occupancy.
