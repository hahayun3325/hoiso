## Important overlap limitation

The overlap pixels are not reliable 3D contact geometry.  A monocular point map stores one visible depth per pixel, so an object/hand overlap pixel can reconstruct the foreground hand.  They are retained only as a 2D occlusion/ownership holdout and never attract the object during fitting.

## Transform contract

The trusted Gate-A laptop is transformed directly into fresh MoGe frame `I` with one proper positive uniform similarity:

`x_I = s R x_G + t`, with `s > 0` and `det(R) = +1`.

Reflections, anisotropic scaling, per-part scaling, topology changes, hinge changes, and independent lid/base transforms are forbidden.  Official ICP may propose seeds, but a lowest nearest-neighbour residual cannot select the final transform.  Exact-camera silhouette, boundary, visible depth, lid/base orientation, hand-only exclusion, and visual review own acceptance.
